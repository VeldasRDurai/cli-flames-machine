# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to actively maintaining a community where all participants feel safe, valued, and encouraged to contribute.

---

## 1. Our Standards of Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* **Be Welcoming and Inclusive:** Recognize that people from diverse backgrounds and experiences bring immense value. Be open to different ideas, perspectives, and interpretations.
* **Use Inclusive Language:** Use gender-neutral pronouns when uncertain of a person's identity. Avoid offensive or exclusionary terms, jokes, or slurs.
* **Be Respectful of Differences:** Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences. Disagreement is normal; disrespect is not.
* **Graceful Criticism:** Give and receive constructive criticism gracefully. Focus on the code, feature, or project structure, not the individual. When correcting someone, offer concrete examples and suggestions for improvement.
* **Show Empathy:** Be kind, patient, and polite in all interactions. Understand that communication styles vary and that written communication can lack tone. Assume good intent.
* **Stay Focused:** Keep discussions relevant to the project goals. If a discussion veers off-topic, gently redirect it.
* **Proper Attribution:** Acknowledge the **intellectual property and contributions** of others accurately, including code, documentation sources, and significant ideas, in a timely manner.

---

## 2. Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

### 2.1 Harassment and Discrimination

* **Offensive Comments:** The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
* **Trolling or Insulting/Derogatory Comments:** Personal attacks, sustained interruption of discussions, or other forms of public or private harassment.
* **Discriminatory Language:** Any comments relating to age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.
* **Publication of Private Information:** Sharing others' private information, such as physical addresses, email addresses, or phone numbers, without their explicit permission (**"doxing"**).
* **Threats:** Threatening or inciting violence toward any individual, including encouraging suicide or self-harm.

### 2.2 Unprofessional Conduct

* **Excessive Swearing or Obscenity:** Using highly inappropriate language in public forums, issues, or pull requests.
* **"Gatekeeping":** Discouraging participation or **dismissing contributions** based on a contributor's experience level, background, or ability to contribute to certain areas of the project.
* **Feigned Ignorance:** Acting purposefully obtuse or avoiding relevant technical questions in a manipulative way.

---

## 3. Scope and Application

This Code of Conduct applies **both within project spaces** and **in public spaces** when an individual is representing the project or its community.

Examples of project spaces include:

* **Online:** GitHub repositories (issues, pull requests, discussions), chat channels (Slack, Discord, etc.), and official project email correspondence.
* **In-person:** Any public events, meetups, or conference talks where project participants are present.

Examples of representing the project include using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## 4. Enforcement and Reporting

We take all reports of Code of Conduct violations seriously and are committed to clear and fair enforcement.

### 4.1 Reporting a Violation

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, or have any other concerns, please report it immediately to the Project Maintainers at:

**Primary Contact Email:** **[report@cli-flames-machine.com](mailto:report@cli-flames-machine.com)** \
**Maintainer Lead:** [`Veldas R Durai`](https://github.com/VeldasRDurai)

**In your report, please include:**

1.  **Your Contact Information:** (Name and preferred communication method).
2.  **The Identity of the Offender:** (If known, including their GitHub handle or name).
3.  **The Time and Location:** When and where the incident occurred (e.g., "June 1, 2025, in GitHub issue #42").
4.  **Detailed Description:** A detailed account of the incident, including any direct quotes or links to public documentation (screenshots are very helpful).
5.  **Witnesses:** The names or handles of any individuals who may have witnessed the incident.

**Note:** All reports will be handled with discretion and anonymity for the reporter will be maintained unless absolutely required otherwise by law.

### 4.2 Enforcement Guidelines

Project Maintainers who do not have a conflict of interest will review all reports and determine, in good faith, the appropriate response.

#### 1. Correction

* **Result:** A private, written warning from the Project Maintainers.
* **Applied when:** The behavior is deemed inappropriate or unprofessional, often a first-time, minor offense. This action may include **locking the relevant discussion thread or editing the offending contribution.**

#### 2. Warning and Temporary Ban

* **Result:** A public or private warning, followed by a time-limited ban from any participation in the project community (e.g., 7 days).
* **Applied when:** There is a clear pattern of problematic behavior, or a single incident is deemed moderate (e.g., repeated aggressive disagreement, sustained harassment).

#### 3. Permanent Ban

* **Result:** Permanent exclusion from any public interaction within the project (e.g., GitHub organization, chat channels) and revocation of all official roles or permissions.
* **Applied when:** An individual engages in sustained, blatant harassment, or commits a serious violation, such as violence, threats, or discrimination.

The maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
